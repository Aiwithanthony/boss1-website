FAQ

Questions, answered

What do you fix?

Batteries, alternators, starters, programming, car scanning and diagnostics, air conditioning, hybrid systems, brakes, engine repairs, servicing and tyre changes. If it’s not on the list, ask.

Do you work on all makes?

We work on a wide range of makes and models. Tell us yours when you book and we’ll confirm.

What parts do you use?

Quality, reliable parts from trusted brands such as Bosch, Denso and KYB.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the car and the job. We’ll talk you through the likely cost and any call-out fee before we start; the final price depends on what we find and the parts needed.

Can every job be done on site?

Not always. Some repairs need a hoist, specialist equipment or parts on order. If yours does, we’ll tell you upfront.
