FAQ

Scanning & diagnostics: questions

Still unsure? Call 0413 404 040

Can you scan any car?

We scan a wide range of makes and models. Tell us the make, model and year when you book.

Does a fault code tell you which part to replace?

Not always. Codes point to a system or condition, so we test further before calling a part faulty.

Can you clear the check engine light?

Yes, once the cause is fixed. Clearing a warning without fixing the fault isn’t a repair.
